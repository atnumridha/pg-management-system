# Product Context

## Why This Project Exists
The Paying Guest Management System aims to solve operational inefficiencies in managing PG accommodations. Traditional workflows for tenant on-boarding, property/room allocation, rent tracking, and payment processes are error-prone and lack digital oversight.

## Problems It Solves
- Centralizes property, tenant, room, and payment information.
- Reduces manual work for admins and staff.
- Automates allocation, invoicing, and payment tracking.
- Provides analytics to aid in operational decisions.
- Enhances data security and reporting.

## How It Should Work
- Admins manage properties, rooms, and tenants via an intuitive dashboard.
- Room allocations, invoicing, and payment processing are streamlined and automated.
- Tenants have a transparent record of their information and payments.
- Secure login with role-based permissions for admins, staff, and tenants.

## User Experience Goals
- Minimize friction for all users.
- Clean, responsive UI for fast access to data.
- Low learning curve for operators.
- Reliable, scalable backend for 24/7 availability.
